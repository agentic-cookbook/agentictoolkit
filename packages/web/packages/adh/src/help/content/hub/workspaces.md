# Workspaces & account

Switch between your personal workspace and any organization or team you belong to,
and create a new organization from Home.

- **Settings** — appearance, account, security, subscription, your public profile,
  notifications, and **API tokens**.
- **Members** — an organization's people roster.
- **Tokens** — mint, list, and revoke storage-access tokens; each one gets its own
  default bucket.
