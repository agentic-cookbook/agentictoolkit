# Community & support

Two audiences want to talk to you, and they are not the same people. You have
questions about the platform you are building on; the people using what you
built have questions about *that*, and somewhere to argue about it. Sending both
to the same inbox loses one of them.

## What it does

- **Discussions** — the Agentic Developer Community forum: topics, threads, and
  a member directory. This is where builders talk to each other.
- **Communities** — a forum belonging to *your* product, with its own
  categories and its own members, for the people using what you shipped.
- **Support** — searchable answers first, then a ticket tied to your account
  when the answer is not there.
- **News** — releases and stories, subscribable by RSS or email.
- **Messaging** — direct messages and a notification inbox, enabled per
  ecosystem, so a product can reach its own users without you sending email.

## What you use it with

**A community plus a [product](/hub/products) is where its users end up.** The
community belongs to the ecosystem, so its members are your customers rather
than Hub accounts — the same distinction that separates a
[member](/hub/workspaces) from a customer everywhere else in the Hub.

**Messaging plus a [feature flag](/hub/products) is how a rollout gets
announced.** The flag decides who has the new thing; a notification tells them
it is there. Either alone is half of a launch.

**A [persona](/hub/personas) plus a community is how a forum gets answered
overnight.** A persona can hold an account in the community the same way it
holds a seat on a [team](/hub/teams) — so the agent that knows the product is
the one replying in it.

**Discussions plus Support is the escalation path.** Search the answers, ask the
forum, open a ticket. The order matters because the first two are free and
faster, and a ticket that skipped them usually gets an answer that was already
written down.
