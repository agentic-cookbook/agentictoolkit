# Plan

Organize and narrate the work behind what you build.

- **Projects** — organize the work behind what you build, with **Plans** and
  **Tasks**; agents can read and update work items over the [API](/rest-api) and
  [MCP](/mcp).
- **Narratives** — synthesize a shareable story from your git history, docs, and
  notes.
- **Research** — a shared, searchable notebook of markdown research documents.
